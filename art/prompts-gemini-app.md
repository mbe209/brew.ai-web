# Brew.ai Artworks: Prompts für die Gemini-App (Nano Banana)

So geht's: In der Gemini-App bei jedem Bild dein Sticker-Sheet mit hochladen, damit Nano Banana die Figuren gleich hält. Dann die Figuren-Beschreibung und den Prompt einfügen, das Bild herunterladen und hier im Thread hochladen.

**Figuren-Beschreibung (immer mitschicken):**

```
Use the three characters from the attached image exactly as they look: the green hop cone character with leafy bracts, big eyes and thin green arms and legs; the golden barley ear character with spiky awns on top; the cream-colored fluffy yeast character with a bubbly texture. Same 3D Pixar-like render style, soft lighting.
```

## 1 · Intro-Hintergrund (hinter „Dein Geschmack. Dein Bier.“)
Dateiname: `01-hero-wide.png` · Format 16:9

```
The three characters standing small at the bottom edge of a wide, almost empty scene, looking up at a giant glowing hop cone floating in the night sky like a moon. Background a smooth gradient from black at the top to deep navy blue (#0B2545) at the bottom, a few tiny stars. Lots of empty dark space in the center for a headline. Cinematic, calm, premium. No text.
```

## 2 · Kapitel „Geschmack“
Dateiname: `24-szene-geschmack.png` · Format 4:3

```
The hop character with eyes closed, tasting a small glass of hazy golden beer, while mango slices, orange zest, passionfruit and pine needles float around its head like a dream bubble. The barley and yeast characters peek in from the sides, curious. Dark navy background with soft blue rim light. No text.
```

## 3 · Kapitel „Ziele“ (das Glas füllt sich)
Dateiname: `25-szene-ziele.png` · Format 4:5

```
The three characters climbing a ladder up the side of a giant beer glass that is half full of golden beer with a white foam crown, the yeast character already sitting on the rim cheering. Dark navy background, warm amber glow from the beer. No text.
```

## 4 · Vorschaubild beim Teilen (Social Media, WhatsApp)
Dateiname: `13-social-card.png` · Format 16:9

```
The three characters side by side, toasting with three different beer cans (orange, amber, lime green) in front of a black-to-navy gradient background, confetti made of tiny hop cones. Room on the left third for a logo. No text.
```

## 5 · Belohnungen: die Founders Box
Dateiname: `26-founders-box.png` · Format 1:1

```
An open cardboard beer box with twelve colorful beer cans, a tall beer glass and a folded t-shirt inside; the hop character proudly presenting it like a game show host, the barley and yeast characters peeking over the edge. Dark navy background, soft studio light. No text.
```

## 6 · Etiketten für die fünf Sorten (später für den Druck)
Format 2:1, je Sorte ein Bild

```
Beer can label illustration for "<Sorte>", featuring <Figur und Szene>. Flat background in <Farbe>, playful but premium, no text.
```

- Citra Comet: hop character skateboarding on a comet made of grapefruit slices, malt orange
- Mango Mood: yeast character in a Hawaiian shirt lounging in a mango hammock, warm amber
- Mighty Malt: barley character lifting a crusty loaf of bread like a dumbbell, sand and orange
- Yummy Yuzu: hop character surfing a wave of yuzu and lime slices, lime green
- Midnight Hop: hop character in a hoodie under a moonlit pine forest, deep navy

**Tipp:** Sticker mit transparentem Hintergrund klappen in der App nicht zuverlässig (sie malt oft ein Schachbrett-Muster). Lieber einen einfarbigen Hintergrund verlangen, z. B. „on a plain flat #0B2545 background“, dann stelle ich frei.
